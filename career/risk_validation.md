## The reasoning under test

Tu's chain, as stated on 2026-10-29:
- The root worry is how stable the income will be.
- The DM team builds, maintains and enforces the SDLC, but no step of the SDLC includes a DM. The role watches the process from outside.
- Making the system better leads toward a system that runs by itself, and that removes the role. The VP of Engineering has described this vision several times.
- Validation, execution and measurement of improvements go to the dev teams or senior leaders, so the role teaches little and has little impact.
- So the role mostly buys money and time, and that fuel runs out. The weight of an exit then lands on the personal goal (the game), and anxiety rises whenever the game stalls.
- Outside options are not believed: PM work looks exposed to AI, a system-builder path like the VP's has no proof, and another career or a business has no base.
- So no way out is visible.

Tu does not ask for certainty. A strategy that can be believed is enough to act on. This file tests the inputs that strategy would rest on.

## Risks the reasoning does not cover

These come from a pre-mortem, still to be run by Tu: it is 2030 and the family income has collapsed; write five stories of how, then rank them by likelihood and damage.
- **OL itself:** an acquisition, a downturn, or AI disrupting the DAM market. Layer 2 sets this aside as "deferred". It may be bigger than the DM role going away.
- **Concentration:** one income, one employer, one country's job market.
- **A pay cut rather than no job:** if OL pays above the local market, the real loss may be a lower-paid next job.
- **Skill drift:** years at 70% capacity while others grow fluent with AI. Phase 1's own conclusion was being outcompeted, not displaced.
- **The cost of the anxiety itself:** it already taxes the game, mood and presence at home, whatever happens to income later.
- **The ex-DM friend's own path:** why the friend left and where the friend went is a base-rate data point.

For each risk still standing: months of warning + months of savings buffer (`financial/financial_checklist.md`) compared with the months needed to respond. If warning plus buffer is longer, the risk is real but manageable. If not, it is the dangerous kind.

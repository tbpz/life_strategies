Which kindergarten for August 2027?

Sakura Kids is the first pick on paper, with Hooray Kids as the backup. The brochures cannot show how teachers treat a crying child, so this page ranks which schools to visit first. It does not close the choice.

Most is known

The only brochure with class sizes (15 to 20 kids with 3 teachers), a timetable for 2 to 3 year olds, this year's fees and the enrolment steps.

A play-first day

Sensory play, clay and stirring, outdoor time every morning. Children set the table, tidy their sleeping place and take a daily duty role.

Kindest sick-day terms

After 10 or more sick days in a row, 20% of next month is covered. Up to 2 months of leave a year. Open until 18:00.

Ask about one rule before paying anything at Sakura Kids

The fee sheet says parents are asked not to share "unverified information in any form", and the school may end its service at once if they do. The biggest worry in the kindergarten plan is a child too young to say what happened. Parents therefore need to be free to raise a concern. If the school cannot explain this rule in plain words, Hooray Kids becomes the first pick.

Only these four private schools are compared. Public kindergartens are not in the brochures, and "decide school type" is still an open item in the plan (due Dec 2026).

Ask at each visit

Questions that come from gaps and oddities in each brochure. A school will say "yes, of course" to a general question, so the ones below ask for numbers and wording.

Sakura Kids
  1. What exactly does the rule on "unverified information" cover? Can a parent raise a worry about a teacher, or ask to see the cameras? Ask for the wording in writing.
  2. Is "20 kids, 3 teachers" the class limit or today's headcount? Who stays with the 2 to 3 year olds all day?
  3. The words "(under 3 years old)" next to the bilingual track look crossed out. Is the bilingual track really for under-3s, and how long is each English period for a 2-year-old?
  4. Are the 6,000,000 and 6,600,000 tuition amounts per month? The sheet does not say.
  5. Can parents watch the cameras live on a phone?
Hooray Kids
  1. Which class takes a 23-month-old, and what does its day look like? The timetable shown is for a class named "MON 1" with no age.
  2. How many kids and teachers are in that class?
  3. The fee sheet skips section III and has year typos. Ask for a complete 2026–27 sheet. Is the facilities fee paid at registration or in September? The two pages disagree.
  4. How is English taught to a 2-year-old? The week shown has daily word lists such as "I see..." and "I'm thirsty".
  5. What is refunded after 3 to 10 sick days?
Little Stars
  1. What does the day look like for 18 to 36 months, and how many kids and teachers are in the class?
  2. Is 5,900,000 the real 2026–27 tuition? One cell on the sheet still says "2024 – 2025".
  3. The facilities fee is printed as 4,000,000, and a handwritten note says 3,000,000 a year. Which one applies?
  4. What is charged from 17:01 to 17:30? The sheet is silent.
  5. What changes at 36 months, when the bilingual track starts? Does the child move to a more English day?
Twinkle Star
  1. Where is the school, and what is the phone number? Neither is in the photos.
  2. Ask for the 2026–27 fee sheet. The one seen is for 2023–24.
  3. Are meals (60,000 a day) inside the 6,500,000 tuition or on top? Monthly cost is 6.5M or 7.8M depending on the answer.
  4. Class size, teachers per class, camera access, sick-day rules, sibling discount.

At every visit, from the kindergarten plan

  • Crying kids are picked up and comforted, not ignored or put in front of a TV
  • Slow eaters are helped and get enough food
  • Rooms, toilets and kitchen are clean (ask to see the toilets and the kitchen)
  • Count the kids and teachers in the room and compare with the answer given
  • See the camera view working, not just hear that cameras exist

Best time: around drop-off (7 to 8 am) or mealtime, when crying and eating can be seen. A visit with no warning shows more than a planned tour.

Next steps

These follow the dates already in the kindergarten plan.

  1. By Dec 2026

    Set the monthly budget, school type and maximum distance. Ask Twinkle Star for an address and a 2026–27 fee sheet. Ask Hooray Kids and Little Stars for class sizes and a timetable for 2 to 3 year olds. Drop any school that does not answer.

  2. Feb to Mar 2027

    Visit Sakura Kids, Hooray Kids and Little Stars, 2 to 3 weeks after Tet. Go around 7 to 8 am, unannounced if the school allows it.

  3. Mar to Apr 2027

    Pay a deposit at the top choice only after the visit. Hooray's 1,000,000 reservation fee is fully refundable, so it is the cheapest way to hold a second place. Sakura's registration fee is lost if the child does not start.

  4. May to Aug 2027

    Get ready and ease in over about 2 weeks, as set out in the plan. Pay month by month for the first 3 months. Consider a yearly pack once the child has settled and the refund rules for unused months are clear.

How the numbers were worked out
  • 12 months of tuition, 22 meal days a month. Late pick-up is counted on all 22 school days of all 12 months.
  • One-off fees counted: Sakura Kids enrolment, facilities, uniform, learning materials and events (7.02M; the 1,000,000 registration fee is refunded when the child starts). Hooray Kids facilities, enrolment, learning materials, uniform and bedding (5.1M; the reservation fee becomes tuition). Little Stars registration, facilities and uniform (6.18M). Twinkle Star facilities only (1.0M); its sheet lists no uniform.
  • Late pick-up: Sakura Kids 15,000 per 15 minutes after 18:00. Hooray Kids 50,000 for the 18:00 to 19:00 slot, nothing before 18:00 (the timetable ends at 17:30, so this is a guess). Little Stars free to 17:30, then 50,000 / 100,000 / 150,000 in half-hour steps. Twinkle Star 50,000 per started hour after 17:00.
  • Twinkle Star's sheet lists meals inside the tuition box but does not say whether they are extra. Counted as extra; if they are included, subtract 15.8M.
  • Not counted: field trips, Saturday school, extra English licence at Hooray Kids older classes (25 USD a month), price rises.

### NOTES

- Pupils have 1 week of summer break per year (end of June and end of August).
- The break week marks the end of a school year, so the child can travel with family; teachers rest and train and improve their teaching, and the school renovates the classrooms for the new year. So the school still charges full months of regular tuition during the pupils' break.
- For day-care items (meals), the school keeps the credit or refunds it to the pupil during the break.

## IV. TUITION DISCOUNT FOR BROTHERS AND SISTERS

*Source: little_stars_04.jpg*

1. **Who it applies to:** families with 2 or more children at the school. The discounts are:
   - **2nd child:** an extra 5% off the 2nd child's tuition, worked out at the time the school has received the full tuition (tuition already reduced by the prepayment discount).
   - **3rd child onward:** an extra 10% off the tuition of the 3rd child onward, worked out the same way.
2. **Papers proving the children are siblings:**
   - The pupil's birth certificate, or a notarised copy of the household register.
   - Or other valid documents (notarised copies) that can serve as proof, if any.

## V. PAYING TUITION AND OTHER FEES

1. **How to pay:** parents can pay by cash / card / instalments at the school office, or by bank transfer using these account details:
   - Bank: Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV)
   - Branch: Thống Nhất
   - Account name and number: *(not copied — see the photo)* ⚠️Q5
   - Transfer note: write <STUDENT'S FULL NAME – DATE OF BIRTH – MONTH… / YEAR …>. Example: <NGUYEN VAN A – NGAY SINH – THANG 1/2023>
   - Transfer fee: parents pay this part themselves.
2. **Confirmation:** after paying, parents should email a payment order / a photo of the bank transfer slip, or hand a copy to the school's Admissions team, to get confirmation.

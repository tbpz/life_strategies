### 3. DISCOUNTS

For families with 2 or 3 children at the school:
- 5% off tuition in the first year and 10% off in the following years for the 2nd and 3rd child, on top of any other discounts (if any).
- 50% off the yearly facilities fee and events fee for the 2nd and 3rd child.

### 4. GENERAL RULES

- Parents must pay all fees in full before the child's first day.
- Fixed fees (facilities, events, learning materials) are not refunded once paid.
- Tuition, meals and other monthly fees paid after the 10th of the month get 3% added to the total.
- If tuition and other fees are more than 20 days late, the school may refuse to accept the child into class.
- If the child is absent and the school is told through the KidsOnline app before 8:00 am, the meal fee is refunded as a deduction from next month's tuition.
- Leave of absence ("bảo lưu") or holiday leave with advance notice is allowed for at most 2 months per year. Beyond that, the school charges 1,000,000 VND per month (not refundable).
- A child who attends fewer than 5 days in a month pays 50% of tuition; 5 days or more pays 100%. If the child is sick for more than 10 days in a row, with advance notice and a doctor's certificate, 20% of next month's tuition is covered.
- During the summer break (June and July), if parents register for leave (within the maximum of 2 months per year) and tell the school the exact number of days off in advance (even if less than a full month), that month's tuition is charged by the days actually attended. The fixed fees (facilities, events, materials) must still be paid in full and are not refunded.
- Withdrawing from the school:
  - 100% of tuition paid for months not yet attended is refunded if the school is told 30 days before.
  - 90% is refunded if the school is not told 30 days before.
- During summer holidays, public holidays, Tet and other state holidays, tuition is not refunded.
- Tuition is adjusted once every 2 years from the last adjustment, with an increase of at most 15% over the tuition in force at the time.

### 5. OTHER RULES

Questions or feedback should go directly to the teacher, the manager, or the Sakura Kids hotline: 1900 9199.
Parents are asked not to share unverified information in any form. If this rule is broken, the school may end its service immediately and without agreement from the parent.

### 2. Enrolment steps

**Step 01 — Advice and enrolment guidance**
Time: 08:00 – 17:00, Monday to Saturday, at the school office.

**Step 02 — Meeting the child**
- After parents confirm enrolment they get an appointment and bring the child to meet the manager and the class teacher.
- After the meeting, the office tells parents whether the child can join the school, and together with parents arranges a schedule for the child to get used to the classroom (if needed).

**Step 03 — Enrolment papers**
- Time: 08:00 – 17:00, Monday to Saturday, at the school office, at least 2 days before the first day.
- Parents get the school's forms (student file, house rules for parents, authorisation form, handbook for starting at "SK" school, consent/permission form), fill them in and return them to the office.
- Pay the fees as listed (explained to parents beforehand).

**Step 04 — First day, uniform handover, learning supplies**
- Start: after the child is placed in a class, parents bring the child to the office to begin.
- Welcome: the child is given the uniform and learning supplies and is introduced to the class.
